# Certification Research Guidance

## Choosing a certification
Start from the role you want to explore and compare certification requirements against current job advertisements. Consider the exam cost, prerequisites, renewal rules, hands-on expectations, language availability, and whether the credential is recognized by employers in your target location.

## Verify official details
Certification titles, prices, exam formats, prerequisites, and retirement dates can change. Before paying, verify information on the official certifying organization's website. This demo intentionally does not state current prices or promise that a specific certification is required for a job.

## Suggested sequence
1. Learn networking, operating-system, and security fundamentals.
2. Complete a small practical project and document it.
3. Review real job postings for recurring skills and credentials.
4. Compare official certification objectives with your gaps.
5. Decide based on role fit, cost, time, and evidence of employer demand.

## Important limitation
No certificate by itself guarantees an interview, job offer, or salary. Requirements vary by employer, region, and seniority.
